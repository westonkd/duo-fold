Twofold is a two-panel Home screen for Android foldables, made for the Galaxy Z Fold8. It works on other Android phones and tablets running Android 12 or later too.

<b>Features</b>
• The Side Bar: the time, battery and your dock along the edge, folded or open
• The App Library: every app, sorted into categories automatically, with search at the top
• Widget stacks: swipe through stacked widgets in place, with a full page of them beside Home
• Search: swipe down for apps, contacts, settings, quick math and the web, with the engine you choose
• One layout for the cover and inner screens
• Folders, hidden apps, icon packs, your own picture for any app's icon, and layout backups

<b>Private</b>
Twofold doesn't request internet access, so your layout, settings and suggestions never leave the device. There's no account and no ads or analytics.

<b>Optional permissions, explained before you grant them</b>
• Notification access: badges on app icons and what's playing. Notification content is never saved or sent.
• Twofold gestures & overlays (an accessibility service): opens Android's notifications and Quick Settings from Home gestures, runs the system actions you give a gesture or button (Home, Back, Recents, close notifications, Android's app drawer, lock screen, screenshot, power menu, split screen, play/pause), and shows the dock and Big Buttons over other apps if you turn them on. It can't read your screen or what you type, and it collects no data.
• Usage access: better app suggestions and dots beside recently used dock apps.
• Contacts and calendar: contact search and the Up Next widget.
• Do Not Disturb access: gesture and button actions that turn Do Not Disturb on or off.
Twofold works without any of them.
